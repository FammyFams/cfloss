Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

cfloss.com is the waitlist page for cfloss, an upcoming floss pick. This page explains what we collect when you join and what we do with it.

What we collect

Only the email address you type into the sign-up box. We don’t ask for your name or anything else.

How we use it

To email you when cfloss launches. We never sell, rent, or share your email address, and we don’t send you anyone else’s marketing.

Where it’s stored

Sign-ups are stored in Google Forms and Google Sheets in our Google account. Google handles that data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies and tracking

cfloss.com doesn’t use cookies, analytics, or ad trackers. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits, including your IP address, for security (GitHub Privacy Statement).

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to remove it, or until we no longer need it for the waitlist.

Removing your email

Email cflosspick@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we’ll delete it from the list.

Children

The waitlist isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their email addresses.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
